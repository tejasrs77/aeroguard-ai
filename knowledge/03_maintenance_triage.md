# Reliability Triage Playbook

This playbook demonstrates decision-support workflow only. Qualified personnel must use approved organizational and regulatory procedures.

## Critical and high risk review

Prioritize critical and high predicted-risk engines for prompt human review. Verify the input history, inspect the influential trends, compare model estimates, and consult approved maintenance records and limits. Record the reviewer, evidence considered, decision, and time. The model must not autonomously ground, dispatch, or clear an engine.

## Elevated and routine monitoring

For elevated risk, increase attention according to approved monitoring policy and watch whether the estimate deteriorates across new cycles. For routine risk, continue normal monitoring. A high RUL estimate is not permission to ignore contradictory inspections, alerts, or domain evidence.

## Human decision authority

AeroGuard ranks attention and summarizes evidence. Final maintenance and operational authority remains with qualified humans operating under approved procedures. If model output conflicts with inspection evidence or required limits, escalate the conflict and preserve the audit trail rather than allowing the model to overrule the human process.
